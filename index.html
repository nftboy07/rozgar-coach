2 · Resume Agent — bullet improver

Recruiters skim for action + what you built + measurable result. Paste a weak bullet and the Resume Agent rewrites it three ways using local rules — strong-verb upgrade, quantification prompts, and a concise version. Replace the [bracketed] numbers with your real ones.

💡 Honesty rule: never invent numbers. If you don't know a metric, measure it (users, pages, marks, time saved) or use the version without one — a true bullet always beats a fake one in interviews.

3 · Interview Agent — practice Q&A

Pick your role, think your answer out loud first, then reveal the model points and rate yourself honestly. Questions cover the mix Indian entry-level panels actually ask: concepts, practical tasks, and HR rounds.

Self-ratings are saved on this device. Aim to reach “Confident” on every question before your interview week.

4 · Tracker Agent — application tracker

Most first-time seekers lose offers to disorganisation, not skill. Log every application, update its status, and let the Tracker Agent show your funnel. Saved only in your browser (localStorage) — private by design.

Why this, why now

India's first-time tech job seekers — especially outside the metros, in Tier-2/3 towns and government colleges — rarely fail for lack of talent. They fail for lack of a system: they don't know which skill to learn next, their resumes undersell real project work, they meet interview questions for the first time in the interview itself, and applications scatter across WhatsApp, email and memory. Paid coaching is out of reach; generic global tools assume a laptop, a credit card and fluent English idiom.

Rozgar Coach is that missing system, as cooperating agents in a single free web page: it plans, it polishes, it rehearses, it tracks. It directly serves Track 10 — Tech Employment for Youth: job discovery readiness, portfolio building, apprenticeship/internship preparation, and a stronger hiring signal for first-time coders.
